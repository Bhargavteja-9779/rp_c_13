## 4. What a human must do before submitting
1. Read the whole manuscript and take responsibility for every statement; complete author, funding, competing-interest and contribution statements.
2. Disclose the use of AI truthfully in the manuscript, as the publisher requires. Do not list an AI tool as an author.
3. Confirm in Clarivate JCR that the journal is currently Q1 and indexed; confirm the APC; download the current author guidelines and adapt length, reference style and template.
4. Resolve the items in `citation_audit.md`.
5. Deposit the code in an anonymised repository and insert the link.
6. Decide whether the honest negative real-shift result is something you want to submit to this journal; a venue that values evaluation studies (and has no speed constraint) may be a better scientific fit.

## 5. Self-decision (Phase 42)
**READY FOR SUBMISSION — in the limited sense that every experimentally defensible requirement has been met**: the experiments
are complete and reproducible, every claim is backed by generated evidence, negative results are reported, and the ten review
rounds have no open item that further computation on the available data could close.

This is **not** a statement that acceptance is likely. Risks 1, 7 and 12 are high and cannot be removed without either a
better method or data that do not exist in this study; they were not papered over. No further experiment was identified that
would change the scientific conclusion, so "NOT READY — additional experiments required" would be inaccurate. Acceptance and
the 72-day timeline cannot be guaranteed.
