# Review round 9 — Associate Editor, Journal of King Saud University – Computer and Information Sciences

**Question: would this manuscript survive peer review at this journal?**

| Criterion | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Scope | In scope (machine learning methodology with applications in fraud, intrusion, churn and socio-economic data). |
| Novelty | Moderate. Clear about what is and is not new. Reviewers expecting a new architecture that "outperforms the state of the art" will be disappointed; reviewers who value rigorous evaluation will not. |
| Technical quality | High for an empirical paper: fixed protocol, 18 datasets, five seeds, four learners, paired tests with correction, effect sizes, ablations, robustness, efficiency, error analysis, exact reproducibility. |
| Evidence vs claims | Consistent after revision. Negative results are in the abstract. |
| Completeness | Declarations present with placeholders; data and code availability statements present; supplementary file present. |
| Reproducibility | Code, configuration, raw results and generators supplied; quick mode executed end to end. |
| Clarity | Acceptable; long (≈ 10,900 words of main text, 13 tables, 8 figures). |

## Remaining concerns raised by the editor

| # | Severity | Concern | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9.1 | MAJOR | Length and number of tables may exceed what the journal tolerates; the journal-specific limits could not be verified. | Not shortened pre-emptively. The authors should check the current guidelines; Tables 9, 12 and 13 can move to the supplement without loss. |
| 9.2 | MAJOR | The practical message is largely negative. Some editors desk-reject "method does not help in practice" papers. | Cover letter frames the contribution as a guarded method plus an evaluation that separates the working regime from the failing one. Risk recorded. |
| 9.3 | MODERATE | Double-anonymised review: the manuscript must not reveal identity. | Manuscript contains no author information; a separate `Title_Page.docx` holds the placeholders; the code link is marked "anonymised link to be inserted". |
| 9.4 | MODERATE | AI-use disclosure. Springer Nature requires disclosure of generative-AI use, and an AI tool cannot be an author. | Placeholder in Declarations and on the title page; the human authors must complete it truthfully. This package was produced by an AI agent, and that fact must be disclosed by whoever submits it. |
| 9.5 | MODERATE | Two cited works are 2026 arXiv preprints read only at abstract level. | Disclosed in the citation audit; claims attributed to them are limited to their abstracts. |
| 9.6 | MINOR | Keywords: six, separated by middle dots. | Conforms to Springer practice. |

**Editor's verdict:** send to review; expect "major revision" with pressure on novelty and on the absence of a positive real-world case.
